# Bilingual philosophy-camp resources

The Community Service page presents each resource in two language rows:

- **中文原版 / Chinese original** — original Chinese camp material.
- **English edition / Full translation** — English translation for international readers.

For each game:

- **PDF** is the browser-friendly slide preview.
- **PPTX** is the editable/downloadable presentation and retains the embedded demo video.
- **MP4** is a standalone web-playable version of the demo; English MP4s include English explanatory captions.

The camp booklet is available as the original Chinese PDF and as a 24-page English translation edition. Original authorship and production credits are retained.
