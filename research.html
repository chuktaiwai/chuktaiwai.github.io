Current project

Extending Liu Xiaogan’s methodology of Chinese philosophy

Early-stage project.

This project brings together four connected forum essays written for History of Chinese Philosophy II. Each considers how Liu Xiaogan’s (劉笑敢) methodological framework might accommodate a further case; one examines Wang Bi’s (王弼) commentary on the Laozi (老子). Classmates raised questions about the entries, and both teaching assistants responded to them in detail. Professor Cheng Chung-yi (鄭宗義) then invited me to develop the four essays into a single paper for publication.

Selected course essay

A Laozian supplement to coexistence theory

History of Chinese Philosophy II.

This English-language course essay reconstructs Professor Cheng Chung-yi’s published account of coexistence and asks whether resources in the Laozi might supplement it.

Current interests

At present my reading centres on Tang Junyi (唐君毅) and Mou Zongsan (牟宗三), both honoured at the Spring Memorial ceremony I took part in at Chi Lin Nunnery. I am asking what their accounts of cultural continuity and moral cultivation teach about sustaining philosophical communities today.
